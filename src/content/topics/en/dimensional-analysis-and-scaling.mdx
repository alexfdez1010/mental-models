A number becomes useful when you know what it measures. This course follows quantities through conversions, exponent checks, geometric scale changes and dimensionless comparisons. You will reconstruct complete calculations, then decide which conclusions still need assumptions or observations.

Begin with [First Principles Thinking](/first-principles-thinking). Here we define the algebra and physical vocabulary used by the new examples. Every physical example is a declared model or constructed dataset; the course teaches how to audit its reach.
