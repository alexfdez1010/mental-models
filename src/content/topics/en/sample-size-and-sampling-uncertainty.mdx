Eight rows can represent eight independent observations, two observations copied four times, or a census of the wrong population. Their row counts match; the claims they support differ.

This expert course turns sample size into an auditable argument. You will specify what could be sampled again, calculate complete small sampling distributions, distinguish a procedure's true precision from a precision estimate, and test whether an interval method covers its fixed target as advertised. Exact finite examples keep the assumptions visible.

The prerequisites teach probability, declared confidence and calibration. Here you will add sampling designs, variance, standard errors and repeated-procedure coverage. Every new term is developed with a worked example; no calculus or statistical software is assumed. The final workshop combines dependence, collection cost and stopping rules.
