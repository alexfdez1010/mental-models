A queue can be empty while the only server is busy with a long job. Two traces can use the same amount of service time and still have very different waits. A formula can be correctly evaluated while describing a different population from the one you care about.

## What you will learn

**Queueing theory** gives you tools to examine those differences. This course follows individual jobs through arrival, waiting, service and departure. You will calculate complete and clipped presence, connect compatible averages through Little's law and derive the measures of one explicitly assumed stationary model.

The first half uses known finite data. The second introduces Poisson arrivals, exponential services and the stationary M/M/1 model before using its formulas. A final workshop checks a changed observation boundary and a constrained planning menu.
