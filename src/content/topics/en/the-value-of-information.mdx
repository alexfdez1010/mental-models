You are deciding whether to launch a product and considering one more survey. If a favourable result would lead you to launch while an unfavourable one would lead you to stop, the survey could affect the decision. If every possible result leaves the plan unchanged, it adds no decision value in this model.

The **value of information** is the expected improvement in the outcome of a decision made with additional information, compared with the best decision available without it. **EVPI** measures the value of perfect information. **EVSI** measures the value of a real, imperfect test; subtract the test's costs to find its net value.

This expert course combines Bayesian updating, expected value and optionality. You'll calculate the perfect-information ceiling, account for test errors and examine whether further research is worth its price and delay. The comparison depends on the available actions, outcomes and probabilities. Information can also have uses beyond the immediate decision; make those uses explicit rather than claiming that every interesting fact must change today's plan.
