A route can have the higher recorded success proportion inside every workload group and the lower proportion overall. Simpson’s paradox is a warning to reconstruct the denominators, not a reason to discard whichever report you dislike.

This course uses complete fictional export and restoration records. You will derive the weighted-total identity, explain a direction reversal, compare both options under one declared reference and check what happens when a required rate is missing. Two compatible completions of unobserved outcomes show why those counts alone do not identify a causal effect.

Build on [Thinking in Probabilities](/thinking-in-probabilities) and [Bayesian Updating](/bayesian-updating). The course defines strata, reference indexes and potential outcomes before using them. Its finite calculations do not model sampling uncertainty, diagnose real allocation choices or guarantee future performance.

Start with [Which total are you comparing?](/simpsons-paradox-and-aggregation/00-introduction), then work through six teaching lessons and the irreversible final exam. The final teaching lesson is an independent dashboard audit.
