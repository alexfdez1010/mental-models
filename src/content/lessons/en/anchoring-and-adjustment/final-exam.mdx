### Constructed tasks

The earlier job took 20 minutes. For the new job, the stipulated durations are 6 minutes to prepare,12 to hang the signs and 8 to pack and check. They are sequential, without overlap or other work in this exercise: the declared total is 26 minutes. A real job would require checking timings, scope and possible overlaps.

The historical cards come from [Tversky and Kahneman](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.185.4157.1124). The lesson distinguishes the task limits of [Epley and Gilovich](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9280.00372) and the valuation study of [Ariely, Loewenstein and Prelec](https://doi.org/10.1162/00335530360535153).

<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
Anchoring describes how a starting value can influence a judgment. Group medians, product comparisons and individual responses are different kinds of evidence. Test explanations, check the origin and weight of references, and retain each task’s limits when applying the course tools.
</Callout>
