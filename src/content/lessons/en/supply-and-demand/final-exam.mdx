<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
A schedule describes willingness across prices. Its quantity at a price is not automatically a completed sales record. Keep shifts and movements separate, state elasticity and price-limit assumptions, and inspect actual allocations. Payments transfer resources between participants; total gains depend on selected values and opportunity costs. A gain metric alone does not settle fairness.
</Callout>
