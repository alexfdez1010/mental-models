### Reference cards

**Research.** Tversky and Kahneman's published 1973 article cites typical-text counts with **twice** as many words having K third as K initial; the majority judgment favoured the initial position. Their famous-name lists had **19 famous names** of one sex and **20 less-famous names** of the other; recall and frequency judgments were measured in different groups. Schwarz and colleagues' published 1991 studies requested **6 or 12 examples** of assertive or unassertive behaviour, assessed experienced difficulty and self-ratings, and varied the interpretation of ease. The first experiment's interaction was marginal; later studies provide additional evidence with their own limits. None establishes a universal individual outcome. [1973 source](https://noorsiddiqui.com/papers/TverskyKahneman.pdf); [1991 source](https://www.uni-muenster.de/imperia/md/content/psyifp/aeechterhoff/sommersemester2012/sozialekognition/schwarzblessstrack_etal.pdf).

**Period comparison.** Two complete four-week parcel records use the same definition: a delivered parcel with at least one complaint within seven days, counted once. Earlier: **10 affected / 1,000 delivered**. Later: **15 affected / 3,000 delivered**. Follow-up is complete.

**Interruption.** In one complete four-week office record: **10 brief incidents × 1 minute** and **1 long incident × 60 minutes**. Types are exclusive, each incident is counted once, and durations are fully recorded.

**Reference cohort.** A complete four-week register contains **100 distinct support incidents**, A=10 and B=90. Presented: A=8, B=9. Valid recalls: A=6, B=3. Each recall is a unique presented ID. Exact realized total inclusion fractions for this cohort are A=3/5 and B=1/30. No subjective ease or judgment was measured.

**Transfer.** A different four-week cohort of **100 distinct support incidents** has valid recalls A=6 and B=3, with **exact known realized total inclusion** A=3/20 and B=1/20. The list has unique presented IDs. These are realized cohort fractions, not random inclusion probabilities.

## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Check the quantity and the evidence">
Retrieval ease, recalled counts and frequency judgments are different measures. Reports and memories can be accurate without representing the target composition. Trace selection, repeated sources and relevant denominators; distinguish a proposed mental or social mechanism from evidence that identifies it. In the finite audit, exact known positive inclusion fractions permit an accounting reconstruction. Unknown filters leave original composition unresolved, and frequency alone leaves consequences and response costs undecided.
</Callout>
