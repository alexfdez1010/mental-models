<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
Start with the quantity that matters and the flows that change it. Trace whether a causal path returns to amplify a change or oppose it, then examine its timing and strength. A loop label is a claim about structure, not proof of a particular outcome.

When choosing an intervention, follow delayed consequences beyond the first report. The print-shop workshop shows why more delivery attempts can coexist with poorer durable progress. Compare the alternative, test pace and quality together, and revise the model when the observed mechanism or return timing differs from its assumptions.
</Callout>
