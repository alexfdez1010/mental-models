## Reference cards

The finite cards below are complete constructed traces. A single uninterrupted, work-conserving FCFS server is empty before time 0. Jobs are admitted in input order for tied arrivals. Each job starts at the later of its arrival and the preceding departure. The observation window is stated in each question. Waiting excludes service; the system includes it.

For a presence interval from arrival a to departure d, observed overlap with [l,r) is max(0,min(r,d)−max(l,a)). Mean occupancy is total observed job·minutes divided by window minutes. Full stays can replace observed durations only with compatible complete-cohort conditions.

The stochastic questions explicitly assume a stationary M/M/1 unless they ask whether those assumptions are justified: constant-rate Poisson arrivals, independent exponential services, one work-conserving FCFS server, unlimited waiting and no rejection/abandonment/rework. With rates per hour and $0<\lambda<\mu$:

- Offered load and stationary busy fraction are ρ=λ/μ.
- L=λ/(μ−λ); Lq=λ²/[μ(μ−λ)].
- Mean total stay T=60/(μ−λ) minutes.
- Mean waiting Tq=60λ/[μ(μ−λ)] minutes.
- πn=(1−ρ)ρ^n and P(N≥k)=ρ^k.

For λ≥μ this M/M/1 has no stationary occupancy distribution or finite stationary means. The offered-load ratio λ/μ remains defined. A finite supplied trace remains a different object. A mean-time criterion does not guarantee a maximum individual time.
