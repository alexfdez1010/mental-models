For the ticket questions, use this **fictional mechanism**. An exporter passes a label to a formatter. In either stage an active setting deletes hyphens; an inactive setting preserves the input exactly. The settings stay fixed. Trusted substitutes preserve exactly what they receive and never restore a deleted hyphen. Fresh inputs and an accurate independent readout are assumed.

## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
Confirmation bias can shape the evidence you seek, interpret and remember. Compare the predictions of genuine alternatives, including combined causes where appropriate. A shared confirming result cannot distinguish those alternatives; an expected result can be useful when rivals predict differently. State validity checks and revision conditions before new results, apply them symmetrically and preserve changes. A controlled deterministic example permits sharper exclusions than noisy evidence, and a successful isolated repair does not establish that the entire system is fixed.
</Callout>
