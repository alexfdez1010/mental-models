This exam tests the complete aggregation audit. Each card is a separate constructed record set. MAIN and CROSSING count export successes per document attempt in their stipulated completed windows; TRANSFER counts passed restoration inspections per sheet in its separate window. Within each card the two named strata are disjoint, exhaustive and compatible across options. Allocation is not stated to be random. Compare options only inside their named card.

For the causal-completion card, the same two hundred MAIN documents each have fixed binary outcomes under A and B. Their chosen-route outcome equals the record and does not depend on other documents’ routes. These are conditions of the constructed worlds, not facts inferred from the observed table.
