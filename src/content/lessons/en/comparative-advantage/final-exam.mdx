## Reference case for the workshop questions

Ada and Bruno each have eight hours. A whole one-hour batch yields Ada four posters or two packs, and Bruno one poster or three packs. Their delivery minima are Ada twelve posters and ten packs, Bruno three posters and fifteen packs. Both compare their final bundle with their best no-trade alternative. Extra posters have value; packs beyond the required quantities do not.

No exchange preserves all eight production hours each. One barter cycle costs one hour each and permits whole posters from Ada in exchange for whole packs from Bruno. The objective is to maximise combined posters while preserving both minima; a strict gain requires each to have more posters than their no-trade alternative.
