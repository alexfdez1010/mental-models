This exam combines resource attributes, private and shared costs, institutional tools and the complete rule audit. Reason from each question’s stated assumptions. A model prediction is not evidence that actual users must behave that way.

<Callout variant="warning" title="How this exam works">
Each answer locks when submitted during this exam. There is no Back, retry or restart. Your score appears at the end; the pass mark is 70%. Some questions require all correct answers.
</Callout>

Reference case for the rule-audit questions: three fixed users, each chooses no additional cow or one. Benefit 6 per addition, loss 3 to **each** user, collected fine 5 on detection, real administration 2 per period. Fine receipts enter a passive fund included with the users; no personal rebate or anticipated personal fund benefit. Users maximize expected personal credits linearly. Detection is known and exogenous, without false positives or collection failures; balances suffice. No rule means fine 0 and administration 0. Amounts are incremental credits over the same period.

## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Check the comparisons">
Identify the resource and its governing rights, compare private and collective increments, and evaluate the actual intervention. Add users and fund without counting transfers as production. Keep real costs, exact ties and omitted mechanisms visible. The finite model teaches an audit; it does not certify sustainable behavior outside its assumptions.
</Callout>
