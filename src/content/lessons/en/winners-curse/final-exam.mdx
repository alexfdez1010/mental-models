## Workshop reference card

All entries are invented. Given our information I, the three joint states are equally likely; we do not see the actual state when bidding. Gross value is common and credits are linear utility. The menu is 0, 75, 95, 120 and 155. Zero abstains, with no lot for us, payment or profit. A positive bid wins only if strictly above R; we lose ties and losing costs nothing. The rival rule stays fixed across candidates and formats.

| State | Gross value V | Rival bid R | Weight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Low | 80 | 70 | 1 |
| Middle | 120 | 110 | 1 |
| High | 160 | 150 | 1 |

The prior mean is 120. First price pays our bid if we win; second price pays R. The initial version has no other cost. The transfer adds a common handling cost of 20, paid once only when we receive the lot. There are no reserves, fees or other constraints. The objective is the largest expected net profit among candidates, including abstention.
