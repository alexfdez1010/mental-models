<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
Regression to the mean is a model-based statistical tendency, not a compensating force or a promise that every extreme will reverse. Check how cases were selected, whether conditions stayed comparable and what would have happened without the intervention. Similarly eligible comparison groups help estimate a contrast; their own changes can include regression, common conditions and chance. Keep measurements and review windows consistent, retain allocation-unit records and assess uncertainty. A useful rollout decision also considers practical value and costs. None of these checks turns an observed contrast into an exact effect, and independent events never become due to compensate for earlier outcomes.
</Callout>
