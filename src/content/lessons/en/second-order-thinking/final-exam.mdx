This final exam brings together direct and mediated effects, behavioural
responses, feedback, uncertainty and proportionate decisions. Read the mechanism
in each scenario rather than using “later”, “unintended” or “harmful” as a
shortcut for causal order. A possible backfire is a claim to check, not a verdict.

<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
Ask **“and then what?”** to expose the links your first account leaves out. Keep
the action and the outcome measure clear. A response can help, offset a gain or
reverse it; its existence alone does not settle the net result.

Treat the map as a set of claims to check. Evidence about mechanisms and a
credible comparison matter more than the number of branches. Match further
analysis to the decision, including possible lasting harm, and use bounded tests
with monitoring and review criteria when they can resolve useful uncertainty.
</Callout>
