## Reference cards

The conversion factors here are exact: one metre is one hundred centimetres; one cubic metre is one thousand litres; one minute is sixty seconds. A temperature’s kelvin value is its Celsius value plus 273.15. A temperature difference has the same numerical value in Celsius degrees and kelvin.

For a positive period $P$, length $\ell$ and acceleration $g$, the proposed monomial is $P=C\ell^a g^b$, with $C$ dimensionless. The period group is $P\sqrt{g/\ell}$. The initial three exact records were $(\ell,g,P)=(1,4,3),(4,4,6),(1,1,6)$ in metre/second-compatible units.

For the positive mechanical quantities in the next table, the groups are $F/(\rho v^2d^2)$ and $\rho vd/\mu$. Adding a relevant acceleration $g$ can introduce $v/\sqrt{gd}$ as another group.

For the filling questions, use an initially empty tank with constant horizontal cross-section and constant declared flows over the stated duration. Net accumulated volume is net flow multiplied by time; height increase is accumulated volume divided by cross-sectional area. All unit conversions and model premises remain part of the account.
