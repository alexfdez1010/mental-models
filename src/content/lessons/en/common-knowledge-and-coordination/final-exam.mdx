Trace who observes what before inferring higher-order knowledge. Knowledge entails truth; shared belief may be mistaken. Public-announcement conclusions need explicit observation assumptions. Specify the timing and failure tolerance of the coordination task, then inspect availability, payoffs and expectations before predicting action.
