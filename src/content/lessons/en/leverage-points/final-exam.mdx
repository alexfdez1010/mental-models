This is the final exam for **Leverage Points**. Treat ordering questions as questions about Meadows’s tentative guide: numbers, buffers, physical structure, delays, balancing-loop strength, reinforcing-loop gain, information flows, rules, self-organisation, goals, paradigms and transcending paradigms. That ordering does not calculate the effect of every intervention. Other questions test specified implementations, outcome and horizon, setup feasibility, direction and conditional comparisons. Numerical questions supply their own inputs; no answer depends on an earlier answer.

<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
Use Meadows’s tentative ordering to generate candidate mechanisms. Compare their implemented responses under an explicit outcome, horizon and feasible menu. A capacity parameter can matter; a label or political resistance alone does not calculate an effect. Preserve ties, check threshold equality and the direction of change, and recompute when relevant inputs change. The related systems models offer distinct mechanisms and assumptions.
</Callout>
