## Model card for the urn questions

These are constructed three-draw models, not empirical data. At each draw choose uniformly among the individual balls currently present, record the colour and return that ball. **Return only** adds nothing. **Reinforcement** additionally adds one new ball of the selected colour after each draw. The main initial urn has 1 red/1 blue; the transfer starts 1 red/2 blue. No ball is removed and no new colour enters. R records red and B records blue; **K** counts red observations in the three draws, not the final physical red share.
