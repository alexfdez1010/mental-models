<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
Leave usable resources beyond a reference requirement where that reserve helps absorb variation or loss. Specify what capacity means, which conditions are covered and when the reserve becomes available. A larger number does not, by itself, establish protection against every failure mode.

Compare alternatives against the same requirement and include the cost of preparation or displaced work. Test simultaneous stresses that can compete for the same reserve. Keep the conclusion conditional on the bounds and shared-dependency assumptions, and review it when the evidence or the operating conditions change.
</Callout>
