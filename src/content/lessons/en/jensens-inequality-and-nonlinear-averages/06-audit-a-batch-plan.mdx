A lower modeled processing total can disappear once you count the work of arranging it. Jensen’s inequality helps compare a response to varying inputs. It does not automatically prepare those inputs for you.

This workshop supplies complete feasible plans, an objective and coordination work. All data are constructed.

Both contain twelve jobs, use four batches and respect the six-job capacity. Their common descriptive mean is three jobs per batch. Here quarter weights describe a complete four-batch catalogue; they are not sampling probabilities.

The even plan’s base work is $4\times 3^2=36$ staff-minutes. The uneven plan’s is $3\times 2^2+6^2=48$. Finite Jensen gives the lower bound four times the square of the common mean: $4\times 3^2=36$.

### When that minimum is the relevant one

The conclusion is about base processing work under the declared response and feasible inputs. It does not yet include coordination. With other arrival restrictions, batch limits or processing responses, the same equal plan might not be feasible or preferred.

## Count the complete alternatives before choosing

The even plan needs eight additional staff-minutes of coordination. The uneven plan needs none. The budget is sixty staff-minutes, and the objective is to minimize complete work among these two listed plans.

Complete work is $36+8=44$ for Even and $48+0=48$ for Uneven. Both fit the budget. Even saves four staff-minutes in this menu, rather than the twelve-minute difference between their base processing totals.

At twelve minutes of coordination, both totals are forty-eight. At thirteen, Even needs forty-nine versus Uneven’s forty-eight. The base inequality remains $36<48$ throughout. Extra work can reverse a complete-plan ranking without refuting convexity.

### When a gap becomes a decision

Check complete alternatives under the same objective and feasibility conditions. A mathematical gap is not an observed causal saving. Implementation costs, changed service or excluded jobs would need to appear in the model before the comparison could cover them.

### When to reuse the model elsewhere

Carry the procedure, not the old numerical difference: fix the inputs and weights, define the response and units, check its domain and shape, compute both orders, and compare complete feasible alternatives under the stated objective.
