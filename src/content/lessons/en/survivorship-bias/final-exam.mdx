<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
Identify the population and the filter before interpreting visible cases. Recover a starting cohort rather than collecting more winners. If outcomes remain missing, keep the denominator fixed and distinguish verified labels, compatible bounds and estimates that depend on a sampling design. An accurately measured cohort rate answers a descriptive question; causal claims need an appropriate comparison and further design assumptions.
</Callout>
