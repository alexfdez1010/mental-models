<Callout variant="success" title="Key takeaways">
State the task population and weights, observed-score metric, information and evaluation count before using a uniform-average tie. A restricted population can favour a method or retain a tie. Observing a one and observing a global maximum are different metrics; adding credit values and unequal query costs creates another comparison. The benchmark supports its declared candidates and assumptions. Extending that claim to a real workload or an everyday analogy needs relevant evidence.
