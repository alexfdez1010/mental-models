### Workshop reference

Constructed one-batch case. Success is worth **120 credits**, failure **zero**. Routine: success probability **0.40**, effort cost **0**. Checking: success probability **0.80**, effort cost **18**. The usual outside opportunity is **10**. Both parties are risk-neutral and know these inputs; payments are nonnegative and enforceable, effort is hidden and output verifiable. Without hiring, the archive receives **zero**. Ties have no selection rule.

| Offer | Failure payment | Success payment |
|---|---|---|
| Flat | 15 | 15 |
| Small | 0 | 20 |
| Edge | 0 | 45 |
| Lean | 0 | 50 |
| Padded | 20 | 70 |

Only for the **participation variation**, the outside opportunity rises to **26**. **Revised** pays **5 on failure and 55 on success**, with all other conditions unchanged.

## Key takeaways

<Callout variant="success" title="Decisions and limits">
Hidden action and private information need different diagnoses. Compare alignment tools with their costs and side effects; predictions depend on assumptions about preferences, observability and permitted payments.

In the workshop, calculate utility under each action, check participation and use the accepted response to calculate principal return. A weak inequality can leave a tie; it does not secretly select the desired behaviour. A fixed payment and a payment spread do different jobs in the risk-neutral model.

Keep a model’s conditional prediction separate from evidence about a real person. A single success or failure does not by itself observe effort.
</Callout>
